These are real .flopkg packages baked into flolower.img at build time
(see tools/build_repo.py and repo/*.txt) and read from disk at runtime
by kernel/ata.c + the download command's FLOPKG parser. There's no
network driver in this OS, so this is a local repo, not the internet —
but the read/parse/install path is real, not simulated.

Add your own: drop a .txt file in repo/, rerun the build, it shows up
here automatically.
